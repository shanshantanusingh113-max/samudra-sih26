The gap

The data already exists. Nobody can look at it together.

INCOIS runs ocean models and INCOIS collects real measurements from robot floats drifting in the same water. Both are valuable, and both already exist. A forecaster who wants to know whether they agree has to open two different desktop programs and hold the answer in their head.

Today

Two windows, flat maps

Model output in one program, float measurements in another, and almost every tool draws one depth at a time. The ocean is four kilometres deep and the structures that matter are vertical.

Today

Agreement judged by eye

Whether the model matches reality is worked out mentally and never recorded. There is no number, so there is nothing to check later or hand to anyone else.

Today

Every new sensor is a rewrite

Adding a mooring, an ADCP or an HF-radar feed means re-engineering the tool, so new observing capability sits unused.

How it works

Three moves, one continuous scene

01 · SURFACE

Start on the globe

India's exclusive economic zone, coloured with INCOIS's own gridded analysis. Every marker is an Argo float that reported in the last 60 days.

02 · DESCENT

Dive into the water

The globe unrolls into a map and the ocean opens into a solid, see-through block. Every coastline vertex slides from its position on a sphere to its position on a flat map: one continuous deformation of one piece of geometry, not a cut between screens.

03 · 2000 m

Click a float

What the instrument measured on the way down, drawn against what the model said at that exact place and time, with the disagreement shaded between them and reported as a number.

How it is built

Open standards in, WebGL out

Rendering

Featureless water is transparent

Ocean temperature falls smoothly with depth, so a plain render is an opaque warm lid over an invisible abyss. Opacity is weighted by how fast the field is changing, so the thermocline and fronts become the solid things in the picture.

Correctness

Land cannot fake upwelling

GPUs interpolate before a shader sees a voxel, so a "no data" marker gets averaged with the sea beside it and paints a cold fringe along every coast. A separate coverage channel means land can never contribute a temperature.

Extensibility

A new instrument is one class

Two Argo providers that name every column differently are absorbed by one parser driven by a column description. The renderer, the API and the UI have never heard of ERDDAP.
